Frequently Asked Questions

Can you migrate our website without taking it offline?

Yes. We configure parallel staging environments and test all functionality thoroughly before performing zero-downtime DNS cutovers.

How does technical SEO differ from regular marketing SEO?

Technical SEO focuses on the code and server layer: site crawlability, JSON-LD Schema markup, response headers, canonical tags, and Core Web Vitals that search engines evaluate directly.

Do you assist with business email setup and deliverability?

Yes. We properly configure SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and MX records to ensure your commercial emails reach recipient inboxes instead of spam folders.

What hosting platforms do you support?

We architect solutions on Cloudflare Pages, AWS (S3/CloudFront/Lambda), DigitalOcean, Hetzner, Netlify, and standard Linux VPS servers.
