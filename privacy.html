Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026 • Effective immediately

At Tech'nSyntax ("we", "us", or "our"), respecting and protecting your personal information is a foundational principle of our business. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your data when you visit our website (technsyntax.site), communicate with our team, interact with our inline AI Chatbot Assistant, or enroll in our technical training programs.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Inquiries: When you contact us via email, WhatsApp, or discovery calls, we collect your name, email address, phone number, company name, and details regarding your technical project requirements.
  • AI Chat Messages: When you send messages to our live AI Assistant on the Contact page, your chat prompt text is transmitted to our production webhook orchestrator to generate an immediate response.
  • Academy Enrollment: Student name, educational background, contact email, and course registration preferences.

1.2 Automatically Collected Technical Information

  • Device & Browser Data: Standard server logs recording IP address, operating system, browser type, referral URLs, and pages accessed to monitor network stability and security.
  • Local Browser Storage: We use client-side sessionStorage to maintain chat continuity (storing an anonymous session UUID and conversation log within your browser). This data never persists across multiple browser sessions and can be cleared instantly via browser history settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

Tech'nSyntax uses collected information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes:

  • To prepare technical proposals, architectural roadmaps, and service estimates.
  • To respond to your inquiries through our AI Chat assistant or direct customer engineering channels.
  • To deliver contracted web development, data engineering, and automation deliverables.
  • To administer educational courses, certificates of completion, and code mentoring reviews.
  • To protect our infrastructure against spam, abuse, automated attacks, and unauthorized access.

3. We Never Sell Your Data

Tech'nSyntax does not sell, rent, trade, or monetize personal information or student data to third-party data brokers or marketing networks under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To operate our secure, high-performance static platform, we partner with reputable infrastructure providers who process data in accordance with modern security standards:

  • Automation Webhooks: Inbound AI chat messages are processed through our private, secured n8n cloud webhook (`anaskhannothing.app.n8n.cloud`) running strict HTTPS TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Content Delivery & Assets: Global CDN networks (Cloudflare, CDNJS) deliver font assets, stylesheets, and iconography.
  • Direct Messaging: WhatsApp business links route communications directly through Meta/WhatsApp's end-to-end encrypted messaging protocol.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We do not use intrusive tracking cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. Our website operates cleanly without third-party advertising cookies. We prioritize privacy-respecting client execution.

6. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security controls, including transport-layer encryption (HTTPS/TLS), role-based access limits, strict password policies, and encrypted credentials storage. While no digital transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% invulnerable, we regularly audit our architecture to mitigate vulnerabilities.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction (including GDPR, UK DPA, CCPA/CPRA, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you hold the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact records.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your project inquiries or student records from our systems, subject to legal compliance requirements.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Opt out of communications or project follow-ups at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and professional services are directed to business professionals and adults seeking technical education. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 16.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our digital services, API integrations, or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.
