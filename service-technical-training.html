faq.training

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Tech'nSyntax courses held live or pre-recorded?

All core programs feature live, interactive cohorts led by active production engineers, complemented by structured reference notes and repository templates.

Do I need a Computer Science degree to participate?

No. Our curriculum is specifically engineered to bridge the gap for self-taught coders and non-technical professionals switching into engineering or analytics roles.

Can you train an entire corporate department simultaneously?

Yes. We deliver customized corporate workshops for teams of 5 to 50 employees, scheduling sessions around operational business hours.

What makes your certificate different from generic courses?

Tech'nSyntax certificates are project-verified: awarded only when a student has authored, debugged, and publicly deployed a working software application reviewed by senior staff.
