Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you prioritize vanilla JavaScript over heavy frameworks?

Vanilla JavaScript does not suffer from framework churn or broken build dependencies. It executes natively in the browser with zero compile overhead, loads instantaneously on cellular mobile connections, and remains maintainable for a decade without forced dependency upgrades.

How long does a typical website development project take?

Most 5-to-10 page corporate websites are designed, developed, audited, and deployed within two to four weeks. Single-page campaign landing pages can typically be shipped in under five business days.

Will my website work properly on phones and tablets?

Yes. Every website we build follows strict mobile-first architecture. We test on compact 320px mobile screens through large 4K displays to ensure typography, buttons, navigation menus, and media scale seamlessly.

Do I own the final source code and intellectual property?

100% yes. Once final payment is settled, all custom code, assets, repository access, and domain configurations belong solely to your organization with zero recurring platform lock-in.
