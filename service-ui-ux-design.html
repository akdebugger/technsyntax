Our Design Methodology

A disciplined product design framework translating business goals into intuitive interfaces.

01

User & Brand Research

We analyze user personas, study competitor positioning, and define visual tone principles (developer-tool clarity, typography).

02

Low-Fi Architecture

We sketch structural wireframes focusing solely on hierarchy, content structure, and conversion paths without visual distraction.

03

High-Fi Prototyping

We design pixel-perfect layouts in Figma, establishing reusable design tokens, typography scales, and interactive states.

04

Engineering Handoff

We annotate exact CSS values, export optimized WebP/SVG assets, and work alongside engineering for 100% implementation fidelity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What design tools do you work in?

Our primary design environment is Figma. We deliver structured component libraries with auto-layout, design tokens, and interactive click-through prototypes.

Do your designs translate cleanly into real HTML/CSS code?

Yes. Because our designers are also engineers, every component is created with real web layout constraints in mind (CSS Flexbox, CSS Grid, clamp() scaling) rather than impractical mockups.

How do you approach mobile responsiveness in design?

We design mobile layouts first (320px–375px), ensuring touch targets are at least 44x44px and typography remains readable without pinch-zooming before expanding to desktop viewports.

Can you redesign an existing live website?

Yes. We conduct a thorough UX heuristic audit of your current site, identify drop-off points, and produce an overhauled design system that lifts conversion metrics.
